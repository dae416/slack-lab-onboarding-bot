# Receipt email template (fictional example note)

Saved 2026-01-20. Use this when you send a P-card receipt to the lab manager (rule: Purchasing_Rules_2026-01-15.md).
Copy the block, fill in the parts in angle brackets, attach the receipt as a PDF or photo.

```
Subject: Receipt - <vendor> - <purchase date>

Hi,

Attached is the itemized receipt for the order below.

Vendor: <vendor>
Purchase date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Amount: <amount>
Project / account code: <project name, account code>
Requested in #lab-orders on: <date of the Slack post>

Thanks,
<your name>
```
