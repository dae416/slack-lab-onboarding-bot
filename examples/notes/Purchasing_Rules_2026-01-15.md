# Example Lab purchasing rules (fictional example note)

Saved 2026-01-15. Source: lab Slack, #lab-orders (posts by the PI, 2026-01-12 and 2026-01-14). Updated 2026-02-03.
Where this note and the handbook disagree, this note is newer.

## Who buys

- Only the lab manager holds the lab P-card (as of 2026-02-03). Do not borrow or share a card number.
- Post every request in #lab-orders: link, quantity, project name and the account code to charge.

## Limits

- Up to $500 per order: the lab manager buys it after your post.
- Over $500, or anything from a new vendor: the PI approves in the thread first (rule posted 2026-01-14).
- No gift cards, alcohol or personal items on the P-card.

## Receipts

- Every P-card purchase needs an itemized receipt within 5 working days of the purchase.
- Email it to the lab manager with the subject line "Receipt - <vendor> - <date>" (template:
  Receipt_Email_Template_2026-01-20.md).
- Lost receipt: fill in the missing-receipt form on the finance office web page and ask the PI to sign it.

## Shipping

- Ship to the lab's building address with "Attn: Example Lab, Room 210"; the front desk keeps packages for 3 days.
