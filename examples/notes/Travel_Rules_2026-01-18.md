# Example Lab travel rules (fictional example note)

Saved 2026-01-18. Source: the PI's post in #general on 2026-01-17 and the finance office's travel page (summarized).

## Before the trip

1. Ask the PI in a DM which conference and which dates; wait for a yes before booking.
2. Submit a travel authorization in the university travel portal at least 3 weeks before departure. Ask the lab
   manager which account code to use for this trip (it depends on the grant; codes change every year).
3. Book flights in the travel portal (economy class). Hotels may be booked directly; keep the folio.

## Meals and per diem

- Meals are paid as per diem at the federal rate for the destination. On the first and last day of travel you get
  75% of the daily rate.
- Meals included in the conference registration are deducted from the per diem.

## After the trip

- Submit the expense report within 30 days of returning, with every receipt over $25.
- Reports filed after 60 days are not reimbursed (finance office rule, as of 2026-01).
